Data Analyst Intern

Northwind Retail is a fictional company used for this demo.

About the role
We are looking for a Data Analyst Intern to join our analytics team in Bengaluru. You will turn sales and customer data into dashboards and insights that help store managers make decisions.

Responsibilities
- Write SQL queries to extract and clean sales, inventory and customer data
- Build and maintain Power BI dashboards and weekly KPI reports for business teams
- Analyse customer behaviour with Python (Pandas, NumPy) and present findings
- Run A/B tests on promotions and measure their impact with statistical analysis
- Automate recurring Excel reports and document data pipelines

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, or a related field
- Strong SQL and Excel skills
- Experience with Python and Pandas for data analysis
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau for data visualization
- Good communication skills

Nice to have
- Experience with Machine Learning or forecasting models
- Familiarity with Git and cloud platforms such as AWS
